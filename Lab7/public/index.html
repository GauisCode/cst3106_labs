How to Play

  1. Roll all five dice
  2. Hold any dice you want to keep
  3. reroll available dice, up to three rolls per turn
  4. Choose on scoring category to finish your turn
  5. Filla ll 13 categories to finish the game

A dash means category is unused. Zero mean unused category but 0 points have been earned for that category

Score at least 63 in the upper section to earn a 35 pooint bonus
